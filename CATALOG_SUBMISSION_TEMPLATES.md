Submit this at: https://gitlab.com/IzzyOnDroid/repo/-/issues/new

```yaml
Name: Secure Dialer
PackageName: com.aistudio.securedialer
Summary: 100% offline, privacy-first FOSS Android dialer & contacts manager
Description: |-
  Secure Dialer is a modern, privacy-focused open-source dialer and local contacts manager.
  
  Features:
  - 100% Offline: Zero internet permissions requested in AndroidManifest
  - Smart T9 Search: Fast phonetic and number dialpad search
  - Local Spam Filter: Regex & prefix-based call screening without cloud lookup
  - Hardware Cryptography: AES-256 SQLCipher encrypted call notes and local backups
  - Fake Call Simulator: Escape awkward situations with scheduled simulated incoming calls
  - Modern UI: Material 3 Expressive design with dynamic colors
```
